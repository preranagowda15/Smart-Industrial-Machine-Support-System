Model Scope & Technical Limitations
1. Fixed Cropping Window [16:112]

The model resizes images to 128×128 and inspects only the central 96×96 area (rows 16–111). Defect scratches or pits positioned near the extreme perimeter or outside the center may be missed if the plate is misaligned.

2. Fixed 0.50 Decision Threshold

The binary decision boundary is locked at defect probability ≥ 0.50. In safety-critical aerospace or automotive applications, a lower threshold (e.g. 0.20) would typically be favored to drive False Negatives strictly to zero.

3. Educational 17-Feature Handcrafted Representation

The classifier computes 17 residual intensity quantiles and pixel gradient statistics fitted with Logistic Regression. It does not learn spatial hierarchies like a Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) or Vision Transformer (ViT).
